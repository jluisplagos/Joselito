- **Crecimiento: dos fuerzas** — *El crecimiento de los beneficios se descompone en inversión en activos nuevos (crecimiento sostenible) y mejora de eficiencia sobre los activos existentes.* “we trace earnings growth back to two forces: investment in new assets, also called sustainable growth, and improving efficiency on existing assets, which we term efficiency growth.” (Growth and Value, p. 2)
- **Crecimiento y valor en el modelo de dividendos** — *Más crecimiento no implica más valor si viene con menor pago o mayor riesgo.* “However, if higher earnings growth is accompanied by lower payout ratios and/or higher risk (cost of equity), it is conceivable that it could lead to lower value.” (Growth and Value, p. 5)
- **Crecimiento y valor en el modelo de empresa** — *El mayor crecimiento solo aumenta el valor si se mantienen fijos la tasa de reinversión y el coste de capital.* “Higher expected growth in after-tax operating income will increase the value of a business, if you hold the reinvestment rate and cost of capital fixed.” (Growth and Value, p. 7)
- **Crecimiento como relato en valoración relativa** — *Un mayor crecimiento no basta para justificar un múltiplo más alto: intervienen el riesgo y la calidad del crecimiento.* “First, while higher growth should be (and usually is) correlated with higher earnings multiples, there are other factors that intervene – higher risk and the quality of growth are two factors that come to mind.” (Growth and Value, p. 8)
- **Proposición 1: subir por la cuenta de resultados** — *El crecimiento tiende a ser mayor cuanto más abajo en la cuenta de resultados (BPA > beneficio neto > resultado operativo).* “growth rates for firms will tend to decrease as you move up the income statement. In other words, earnings per share growth rates will be higher than net income growth rates and net income will grow faster than operating income.” (Growth and Value, p. 14)
- **Tamaño y crecimiento** — *Cuanto más grande es la empresa, menos crece, porque mantener tasas altas es más difícil sobre una base mayor.* “As firms get larger, revenues and earnings grow at lower rates, a phenomenon that can be attributed to two factors. The first is that delivering high growth rates becomes more difficult at higher levels of income (revenue) than at lower levels.” (Growth and Value, p. 16)
- **Proposición 3: escalar es difícil** — *La tasa de crecimiento esperada debe ligarse al tamaño de la empresa y decrecer al aumentar este.* “Scaling up is hard to do. The expected growth rate for a firm should be tied to the size of the firm, with growth rates decreasing as the firm gets larger.” (Growth and Value, p. 17)
- **Higgledy piggledy growth** — *Los estudios clásicos no hallan relación entre el crecimiento de un periodo y el del siguiente.* “examined whether British companies that reported high earnings growth in one five-year period continued to exhibit that growth in the next five-year period and concluded that there was no relationship between growth in the two periods.” (Growth and Value, p. 17)
- **Persistencia en EE. UU. (1951–1997)** — *La mediana del crecimiento de beneficios se parece al PIB, el crecimiento alto es raro y no persiste más que por azar.* “the median growth rate in earnings across firms corresponds closely to growth in Gross Domestic Product (GDP), high growth was uncommon and that there was no persistence in earnings growth beyond chance.” (Growth and Value, p. 17)
- **Matriz de persistencia del beneficio neto** — *Las empresas de mayor crecimiento en un periodo no repiten más de lo que haría el azar.* “For the most part, there is no evidence of persistence. For instance, of the firms that delivered the highest growth between 1997 and 2002, only 17.15% remained in the highest growth class for the 2002-07 time period, not significantly different from the 16.67% you would expect to observe with a completely random process.” (Growth and Value, p. 18)
- **Correlación negativa del crecimiento** — *Tras un alto crecimiento del beneficio es más probable un mal crecimiento posterior (reversión a la media).* “Put more starkly, at least over the two time periods that we examined, firms with high growth rates in earnings in one period are more likely to be underperformers when it comes to growth in the next period.” (Growth and Value, p. 19)
- **Proposición 4: poco valor predictivo del pasado** — *El crecimiento histórico predice mal el crecimiento futuro.* “There is little predictive value in historical growth rates and high growth rates in the past are not indicative of high growth rates in the future.” (Growth and Value, p. 19)
- **Proposición 5: previsiones de analistas** — *Los analistas aciertan algo a corto plazo, pero casi nada en el crecimiento a largo plazo.* “Analyst estimates of earnings (and growth) have some predictive value for short-term earnings forecasts, but are of little or any value for long-term growth forecasts.” (Growth and Value, p. 24)
- **Determinantes del crecimiento: ROI estable** — *Si el retorno de la inversión no cambia, el crecimiento depende solo del retorno y de cuánto se reinvierte.* “Consider the simplest scenario, where the ROI is stable and does not change from period to period” (Growth and Value, p. 28)
- **Descomposición del crecimiento: inversión nueva** — *El primer término del crecimiento es el retorno marginal de la nueva inversión por la proporción reinvertida.* “The first term in this equation captures the growth from new investments, determined by the marginal return on those investments and the proportion invested in these investments.” (Growth and Value, p. 28)
- **Única fuente de crecimiento sostenible** — *Con retornos estables sobre los activos existentes, la inversión nueva es la única fuente de crecimiento; y siempre la única a largo plazo.* “for a firm with stable returns on existing assets, this is the only source of growth and for all firms, it the only source of long term sustainable growth.” (Growth and Value, p. 30)
- **Retorno marginal frente a medio** — *El retorno de lo nuevo es marginal; el de lo existente es una media, y pueden diferir mucho.* “The return on existing assets is an average return on a portfolio of investments already made. While we often use the same value for both numbers in valuation, they can be different, in fact, very different in practice.” (Growth and Value, p. 30)
- **Proposición 7: crecimiento fundamental** — *El crecimiento sostenible depende solo de la tasa de reinversión y del retorno de lo reinvertido.* “The long term (and sustainable growth rate) for a firm is a function of only two variables – the proportion of earnings that are invested back into the business (the reinvestment rate) and the returns earned on these investments.” (Growth and Value, p. 31)
- **La misma reinversión rinde distinto** — *La misma tasa de reinversión rinde más crecimiento donde el retorno del capital es mayor.* “the same reinvestment rate provides a much bigger bang for the buck in some companies (with high returns on capital) than it does in others.” (Growth and Value, p. 31)
- **Crecimiento por eficiencia: más potencial con retorno bajo** — *Una mejora del retorno sobre un nivel bajo produce mucho más crecimiento que sobre un nivel alto.* “A firm that improves its return on capital from 5% to 6% will report a 20% growth rate from efficiency in that period, whereas a firm that improves its return on capital from 25% to 26% will generate a 4% growth rate from efficiency in that period.” (Growth and Value, p. 32)
- **Eficiencia: duración finita** — *La eficiencia solo justifica crecimiento durante un tiempo limitado; después se vuelve al crecimiento sostenible.* “You can draw on increased efficiency to justify growth only for finite periods. After all, a firm cannot be infinitely inefficient. Once the inefficiencies, no matter how significant, are fixed, the firm will have to revert back to its sustainable growth rate, based upon new investments.” (Growth and Value, p. 32)
- **Eficiencia en el valor terminal** — *En el periodo de alto crecimiento valen eficiencia e inversión nueva; en el valor terminal, solo la inversión nueva.* “you can draw on both efficiency and new investments to justify growth during the high growth period, but only on new investments to justify growth forever (in the terminal value computation).” (Growth and Value, p. 32)
- **Las ganancias de eficiencia se agotan** — *Cuando se agotan las mejoras de eficiencia, el crecimiento cae hacia el sostenible; no se puede seguir retirando capital.* “As efficiency gains run their course, earnings growth rates will decline towards the sustainable growth rate. In other words, firms cannot continue to draw down capital and expect to keep growth at healthy levels.” (Growth and Value, p. 33)
- **Crecimiento que rebota** — *El crecimiento de las empresas rebota con poca predictibilidad; alto o bajo hoy no anticipa el siguiente periodo.* “Growth rates at companies bounce around, with little predictability. Firms that generate high growth in one period are just as likely to be low growth as high growth firms in the next period.” (Growth and Value, p. 34)
- **El tamaño frena reinversión y retorno** — *Para crecer mucho hay que reinvertir mucho con retorno alto, y el tamaño juega en contra de ambas cosas.* “For a firm to grow at a high rate, it has be able to reinvest a large proportion of its earnings, while maintaining a high return on capital. On both dimensions, size works against companies.” (Growth and Value, p. 34)
- **Decaimiento hacia el crecimiento de la economía** — *Las tasas de crecimiento se desvanecen deprisa hacia las de la economía.* “Across companies, growth rates fade towards that of the economy quickly. For instance, the real revenue growth rate of the companies that were in the S&P 500 in 1969 decreased from 7% in that year to the GDP growth rate of 4% in 1974 and down to 2% in 1979” (Growth and Value, p. 35)
- **Proposición 9: duración del alto crecimiento** — *Crecer por encima de la economía durante diez años o más es la excepción.* “Firms that grow at rates higher than the economy for extended periods (10 years or more) are more the exception than the rule.” (Growth and Value, p. 35)
- **Retorno igual al coste de capital** — *Si el retorno de la nueva inversión iguala el coste del capital, el crecimiento es neutro para el valor.* “In the special case where the return on equity is equal to the cost of equity, growth has a neutral effect on value, with the positive effect of higher growth exactly offset by the negative effect of lower cash flows.” (Growth and Value, p. 37)
- **Versión de empresa: ROIC frente a coste de capital** — *Con ROIC igual, mayor o menor que el coste de capital, crecer no crea, crea o destruye valor.* “A firm that generates a return on capital equal to its cost of capital will create no value as it increases growth, whereas a firm that is able to earn more (less) than its cost of capital will increase (decrease) value as it increases growth.” (Growth and Value, p. 37)
- **Proposición 10: crecimiento con exceso de retorno** — *No es el crecimiento en sí, sino el crecimiento con retornos excedentes positivos el que crea valor.* “It is not growth, per se, that creates value but growth with positive excess returns. Higher growth can add value, destroy value or leave value unchanged.” (Growth and Value, p. 37)
